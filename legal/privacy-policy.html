Privacy Policy

IndieMark is a browser-based prototype. Selected media is processed locally by the browser in the current version and is not intentionally uploaded to an IndieMark server.

Local storage

Distribution records, recipient labels and investigation records may be stored in IndexedDB on this device. Clearing browser data can remove them. Export a backup if you need a separate copy.

Third-party services

The site may load web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact that provider to retrieve fonts.

Limitations

This prototype has not undergone an independent privacy or security audit. Do not store sensitive personal information in it.
